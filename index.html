遠端控制Remote control

反過來：用手機操控電腦The other way round: control the PC from your phone

手機的瀏覽器（iPhone 用 Safari、Android 用 Chrome）就能看到電腦畫面、聽到電腦的聲音，並用手指操控滑鼠和鍵盤，手機不用裝 App。在家掃 QR 碼；人在外面，用固定網址加密碼。預設關閉，在主視窗的「遠端控制」分頁打開。A phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android) shows you the PC's screen, plays its sound and lets you control the mouse and keyboard with your fingers, with no app on the phone. At home, scan a QR code; away from home, use a fixed address and a password. Off by default: turn it on in the Remote control tab of the main window.

在家At home

同一個 Wi‑Fi・掃 QR 碼Same Wi‑Fi · scan a QR code

  1. 電腦主視窗切到「遠端控制」分頁，打開開關，按「在家（同一個 Wi‑Fi）」的「顯示 QR 碼」On the PC, open the Remote control tab, turn it on and press Show QR code under At home (same Wi‑Fi)
  2. 手機用相機掃 QR 碼，瀏覽器就打開了Scan the QR code with the phone's camera; the browser opens
  3. 第一次，電腦上會問你「允許」，按下去就開始The first time, the PC asks you to Allow; press it and you're in

在外面：固定網址＋密碼Away: fixed address + password

Tailscale Funnel・推薦Tailscale Funnel · recommended

  1. 電腦按「設定密碼」（至少 8 個字），再按「開啟固定網址」；勾「開機自動開啟固定網址」就能開機後直接從外面連On the PC press Set password (8+ characters), then turn on the fixed address; tick Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts to use it right after the PC starts
  2. 在外面用手機瀏覽器打開固定網址（可按「複製網址」，或掃它的 QR 碼）Away from home, open the fixed address in the phone's browser (Copy address, or scan its QR code)
  3. 輸入密碼就連上了，不用先碰電腦；這支手機 30 天內不用再輸入Enter the password and you're in, nothing to do on the PC first; that phone needn't ask again for 30 days

臨時網址Temporary address

免安裝・偶爾用一次Nothing to install · for the odd time

  1. 電腦「遠端控制」分頁選「臨時網址」，畫面出現一次性 QR 碼和 6 位數通行碼On the PC pick the Temporary tab: a one-time QR code and a 6-digit passcode appear
  2. 手機掃 QR 碼（只能用一次），輸入通行碼Scan the QR code (it works once) and enter the passcode
  3. 電腦按「允許」（臨時網址每次都要按，不能記住）Press Allow on the PC (needed every time with a temporary address; it can't be remembered)

給朋友用：你自己只要輸入密碼。要讓別人暫時連進來，按「新增臨時帳號」，選有效時間（用一次到 7 天）、可操作或僅觀看、能不能傳檔，再按「複製全部」把網址和密碼傳給對方。網址已經帶上帳號（在 # 後面），朋友打開後也只要輸入密碼；連上時你在電腦按「允許」，到期自動刪除（需先開固定網址）。手機也裝 Tailscale 的話，還能「像在家一樣」連；臨時網址不用裝任何東西，由程式附帶的 cloudflared 轉送。圖中的網址、手機內容都是示範用的虛構資料。For a friend: you only type the password yourself. To let someone in for a while, press Add a temporary account, pick the validity (once to 7 days), operate or view only, and whether files are allowed, then use Copy all to send them the address and password. The address already carries the account (after the #), so your friend also only types the password; you press Allow on the PC when they connect, and the account is deleted when it expires (the fixed address has to be on). With Tailscale on the phone too it works like at home; the temporary address needs nothing installed (the program bundles cloudflared). The addresses and phone content in the images are made up.

手指操控和觸控板Finger control and trackpad

「直接點」點哪裡按哪裡（預設）；「觸控板」像筆電：單指移動、點一下左鍵、雙指點右鍵、雙指捲動、捏合縮放。三指點一下叫出鍵盤，也有 Ctrl／Alt／Win 組合鍵和雙向剪貼簿。Direct clicks where you tap (the default); Trackpad works like a laptop: one finger moves, tap to click, two-finger tap to right-click, two fingers to scroll, pinch to zoom. A three-finger tap opens the keyboard; Ctrl / Alt / Win combinations and a two-way clipboard too.

電腦聲音傳到手機The PC's sound on the phone

電腦在播什麼，手機就聽到什麼；聲音會自動調到適當大小，手機音量最高 400%。想要的話，勾「手機播聲音時電腦靜音」。Whatever the PC plays, the phone plays; the phone volume goes up to 400%, levelled automatically. Tick Mute the PC while the phone plays sound if you like.

雙向傳檔Files both ways

手機傳到電腦：存進「下載\自在投影遠端」，單檔最大 2 GB；「僅觀看」時不能傳檔。電腦傳到手機：在檔案總管複製檔案，手機按「重新整理」就能下載。Phone to PC: saved in Downloads\自在投影遠端, up to 2 GB each; no file transfer in View only. PC to phone: copy files in File Explorer and tap Refresh on the phone.

電腦端提示列和 Ctrl+DThe bar on the PC, and Ctrl+D

連線期間，電腦螢幕上方一直顯示「正在被遠端控制」與連線來源；按「中斷」或 Ctrl+D 隨時切斷。從外面連線的手機斷線後，電腦可以自動鎖定。While a phone is connected a bar at the top of the PC screen says Remote control in progress and where it comes from; press End or Ctrl+D to cut it any time. When a phone connected from outside drops, the PC can lock itself.

安全Safety

  • 預設關閉。要你自己打開；連線時電腦上方有提示列，隨時能用「中斷」或 Ctrl+D 切斷。
  • 新手機要在電腦上按「允許」。密碼輸錯會依來源位址限制，不會連累別人；電腦只存加密過的雜湊。
  • 只用你自己的 Tailscale 帳號，沒有我們的伺服器；只有選「臨時網址」才經過 Cloudflare。一次只能一支手機，不能操作系統管理員視窗、UAC 和鎖定畫面。
  • Off by default. You turn it on yourself; a bar on the PC shows while connected, and End or Ctrl+D cuts it at any time.
  • A new phone has to be allowed on the PC. Wrong passwords are limited per source address without affecting anyone else; the PC stores only a salted hash.
  • Only your own Tailscale account, no server of ours; only the temporary address passes through Cloudflare. One phone at a time; administrator windows, UAC and the lock screen can't be controlled.

隱私Privacy

畫面不離開你家的網路Your screen stays on your network

畫面、聲音、錄影、截圖都只在你的區域網路和電腦裡。沒有遙測、沒有分析、沒有廣告、不用帳號。Picture, sound, recordings and screenshots stay on your LAN and your PC. No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no account.

  • 平常唯一的對外連線是檢查更新。啟動 30 秒後、之後每 24 小時一次，向 GitHub 讀取 update.json，不帶任何裝置資訊。
  • 不會自己更新。只有你按「更新到 vX.Y.Z」，才從本站 /download/ 下載安裝程式，並以 SHA-256 驗證。
  • 可以完全關閉。在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhoneMirror\settings.ini 加一行 update_url=。
  • 畫面翻譯在電腦上離線執行。畫面和文字都不上傳；第一次使用前先詢問，同意後才下載翻譯與文字辨識模型（每個檔案以 SHA-256 驗證）。本機 AI 翻譯也只在你選擇時下載。
  • 線上翻譯預設關閉。只有你開啟、同意並填入自己的 DeepL／Azure／Google 金鑰後，才把辨識出的文字（不含畫面）送到你選的服務。
  • 遠端控制預設關閉。在家時手機直接連這台電腦；新手機要在電腦上按「允許」，連線時畫面上一直有提示。只有你選用時才經過 Tailscale（固定網址需密碼）或 Cloudflare 的臨時網址。
  • 傳到手機只在區域網路。Android 經無線偵錯直接傳，iPhone 掃 QR 碼從這台電腦下載，不經過任何伺服器。
  • The only routine internet access is the update check. 30 s after start, then every 24 h, it reads update.json from GitHub, with no device information.
  • Never updates by itself. Only when you click “Update to vX.Y.Z” is the installer downloaded from this site's /download/ and verified with SHA-256.
  • Can be turned off. Add the line update_url= to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhoneMirror\settings.ini.
  • Translation runs offline on your PC. Neither the picture nor the text is uploaded; the app asks before downloading the translation and text-recognition models (each checked with SHA-256). On-device AI translation is downloaded only if you choose it.
  • Online translation is off by default. Only after you turn it on, agree and enter your own DeepL / Azure / Google key is the recognised text (never the picture) sent to the service you picked.
  • Remote control is off by default. At home the phone connects straight to this PC; a new phone has to be allowed on the PC, and a bar stays on screen while connected. Tailscale (the fixed address needs your password) or Cloudflare's temporary address are used only when you pick them.
  • Send to phone stays on your LAN. Android gets files over wireless debugging; an iPhone scans a QR code and downloads from this PC. No server in between.

FAQ

常見問題Questions

找不到答案？到 GitHub Issues 告訴我們。Not here? Ask on GitHub Issues.

安裝時出現「Windows 已保護您的電腦」？
安裝程式目前沒有數位簽章，請按「其他資訊」→「仍要執行」。也可以從 Release 頁面下載原始碼自己建置。
iPhone 找不到「自在投影」？
  • 手機和電腦要在同一個 Wi‑Fi（不是訪客網路）；學校、公司、飯店網路可能封鎖裝置探索。
  • Windows 防火牆要允許「私人網路」。
  • 先關掉 VPN；同一台電腦只能有一個 AirPlay 接收程式。
可以用電腦操控 iPhone 嗎？
不行，AirPlay 只傳畫面和聲音。用電腦操控只支援 Android（無線偵錯）。
遠端控制要在手機裝 App 嗎？
不用。手機只要有瀏覽器（iPhone 的 Safari、Android 的 Chrome），也可以「加入主畫面」變成像 App 的圖示。電腦要裝自在投影。只有你選「Tailscale」這種連法時，手機才需要另外裝 Tailscale。
人在外面，怎麼連回家裡的電腦？
最推薦「固定網址＋密碼」：在家先在電腦的「遠端控制」分頁設定密碼（至少 8 個字）、開啟固定網址，勾「開機自動開啟固定網址」，開機後就能用；到外面用手機瀏覽器打開那個網址、輸入密碼即可。電腦要裝好 Tailscale 並開啟 Funnel。不想裝的話改用「臨時網址」（免安裝，一次性 QR 碼加 6 位數通行碼，要在電腦旁產生）。兩種都不用在路由器開連接埠。
遠端控制安全嗎？別人會不會連進來？
預設關閉，打開後新手機要在電腦上按「允許」（固定網址則要你設定的密碼）；密碼連續輸錯會依來源位址暫時擋掉；連線時電腦上方一直有提示列，按「中斷」或 Ctrl+D 隨時切斷，也能開「僅觀看」；斷線後可自動鎖定電腦。固定網址和 Tailscale 只走你自己的 Tailscale 帳號，沒有第三方伺服器。仍請設一組夠長的密碼，不要把網址和密碼一起貼給別人。
遠端控制沒有聲音怎麼辦？
  • 手機畫面下方按「開啟聲音」（瀏覽器規定要手動點一下才能出聲）。
  • 電腦主視窗「遠端控制」分頁要勾「電腦聲音傳到手機」；電腦也要真的在播放聲音，不然手機會顯示「電腦目前沒有在播放聲音」。
  • 手機頁面的設定裡，「手機音量」可以調高（最高 400%），也檢查手機本身的音量。
看 Netflix 等影片是黑畫面？
有 DRM 保護的影片在鏡像時會被手機本身遮黑，任何接收程式都無法繞過。
Android 投放（Miracast）不能用？
電腦要安裝 Windows 選用功能「無線顯示器」並重新開機，Wi‑Fi 網卡也要支援（netsh wlan show drivers 顯示 Wireless Display Supported: Yes）。不行就改用無線偵錯。
關掉視窗後程式跑去哪了？
選了「背景待命」時，程式會繼續接收投影，手機連上時視窗自動出現；要自己打開，按工作列右邊的自在投影圖示（沒看到就先按「^」）。要完全結束，在圖示上按右鍵 →「結束」。
延遲或畫面糊？
改用 5 GHz Wi‑Fi 或讓電腦接網路線。延遲優先選「畫面清晰度 ▸ 標準（順暢）」，要清楚選「高」或「最高」。
有 Mac 或 Linux 版嗎？
目前只有 Windows 10 / 11（64 位元）。
真的免費嗎？
是的：GPL-3.0 開源、沒有付費版、沒有廣告、不收集資料。喜歡的話，在 GitHub 按個 Star 就是最好的支持。
“Windows protected your PC” when installing?
The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway, or build from the source zip on the release page.
The iPhone doesn't see Zizai Cast?
  • Same Wi‑Fi (not a guest network); school, office and hotel networks often block discovery.
  • Allow the app on Private networks in Windows Firewall.
  • Turn VPNs off; only one AirPlay receiver can run per PC.
Can I control the iPhone from the PC?
No. AirPlay carries picture and sound only. PC control works for Android (wireless debugging).
Do I need an app on the phone for Remote control?
No. A browser is enough (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android), and you can Add to Home Screen for an app-like icon. The PC needs Zizai Cast. Only if you pick the Tailscale way does the phone also need the Tailscale app.
How do I reach my PC at home from outside?
The recommended way is fixed address + password: at home, on the PC's Remote control tab set a password (8+ characters) and turn on the fixed address; tick Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts so it works right after the PC starts. Away from home, open that address in the phone's browser and enter the password. The PC needs Tailscale installed with Funnel turned on. If you'd rather not install it, use the temporary address (nothing to install: a one-time QR code plus a 6-digit passcode, generated next to the PC). Neither way needs a port opened on your router.
Is Remote control safe? Can strangers get in?
It is off by default. Once on, a new phone has to be allowed on the PC (a fixed address needs the password you set); an address that keeps typing a wrong password is shut out for a while; a bar on the PC screen shows while connected, and End or Ctrl+D cuts it any time; View only is one switch away, and the PC can lock itself when the phone drops. The fixed address and Tailscale use only your own Tailscale account, with no third-party server. Still, choose a long password and don't paste the address and password together.
No sound in Remote control?
  • Tap Turn on sound at the bottom of the phone screen (browsers insist on a tap before they play sound).
  • On the PC's Remote control tab, Send the PC's sound to the phone must be ticked, and the PC has to be playing something, or the phone says the PC isn't playing sound.
  • In the phone page's Settings raise Phone volume (up to 400%), and check the phone's own volume.
Netflix shows a black screen?
DRM-protected video is blacked out by the phone itself during mirroring; no receiver can bypass that.
Android Cast (Miracast) doesn't work?
Install the Windows optional feature “Wireless Display”, reboot, and check your Wi‑Fi adapter (netsh wlan show drivers should say Wireless Display Supported: Yes). Otherwise use wireless debugging.
Where did the app go after I closed the window?
With Keep running in the background, it keeps receiving mirroring and the window comes back when a phone connects. To open it yourself, click its icon at the right end of the taskbar (click ^ if you don't see it). To quit, right-click the icon → Quit.
Lag or a blurry picture?
Use 5 GHz Wi‑Fi or a wired PC. For low latency choose Picture quality ▸ Standard (smoothest); for sharpness High or Highest.
macOS or Linux?
Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) only for now.
Is it really free?
Yes: GPL-3.0, no paid tier, no ads, no data collection. A Star on GitHub helps others find it.
